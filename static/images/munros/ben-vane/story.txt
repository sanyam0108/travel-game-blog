The Staircase of the Arrochar Alps.

By this point, I was fully determined to get on with Munro bagging. This hike was special not just for the climb, but for the company. Trekking up the steep slopes with my pals from Spain and India, we turned the mountain into a language school—practicing Spanish and Hindi all the way to the summit. 

Ben Vane is famous for being the shortest Munro, but it is a relentless "staircase." On my first attempt, the summit was shy and hidden in the clouds with no views to speak of. However, I returned later with Norman on a clear day and was rewarded with some of the best views in the Arrochar Alps. It’s a short, sharp, and steep hike that I’ve come to love, proving that sometimes the best views require a second look!
