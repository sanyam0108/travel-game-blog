The Arrochar Giants.

Before I had a car, these two were my go-to mountains because they are so accessible by public transport. They are unrelenting but incredibly rewarding. On my first big trip here, I bagged both Ben Ime and Beinn Narnain in one long day, even throwing in The Cobbler for good measure. 

The walk starts beautifully through the Arrochar woodlands before the real climbing begins. I’ve been back here multiple times—once even carrying my gear to wild camp on the summit of Beinn Narnain. That sunset remains one of the best I’ve ever seen, though the midges in the summer were a total pain; they definitely have a taste for my blood! 

It’s a long day that requires plenty of water, especially when the sun is out, but looking back at these as my initial Munros, I realize how well I did to tackle them early on.
