If you had asked me that morning, I wouldn't have known what a "Munro" really was. This was the one that started it all.

We headed out as a group of six for an organized work walk. The atmosphere was great—plenty of good chats and laughs as we started the ascent. It was a proper "stairway start," and since it was a beautiful sunny day, it didn't take long before we were all sweating. The climb felt moderately hard at the time, but the views opening up over Loch Lomond kept us moving.

What I didn't expect was the transformation at the top. We left the sun behind and found ourselves in a world of white; there was a thick capping of snow across the summit plateau. Standing there, looking out over the Highlands, something clicked. I realized I didn't just want to do this once—I wanted to do all of them. That was the exact moment the "bagging" journey officially began.

The descent turned out to be the best part. Instead of a tedious walk down, we ended up sliding down the snowy sections on the way back. It was total chaos, completely fun, and we were all laughing like kids. By the time we reached the bottom, I was hooked. One down, 281 to go.
