The Day the Core Group Formed.

Setting off from Inveruglas, the sun was shining in a way that I've rarely seen since in the Highlands. This wasn't just my second Munro; it was the birth of a community. 

Though it’s a shorter hike in terms of distance, Ben Vorlich doesn't give its summit away easily. It is relentlessly steep from the moment you leave the hydro road. But the effort was shared with a phenomenal group—people who started as strangers but became my core walking circle and best friends. It was right here, looking out over the Arrochar Alps, that I decided to officially start my own walking Meetup group.

The views from the top were world-class. Looking back south, I could see Ben Lomond standing proud across the water, a reminder of where I started just a week prior. This hike proved that the Munros are about more than just the peaks; they’re about the people you climb them with.
